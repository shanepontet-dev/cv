Our Q2 anchor asset is a Manufacturing white paper. For edie specifically, we are angling all four assets below around London Climate Action Week 2026 (week commencing 22 June). LCAW is edie's largest event of the year and traffic peaks across every format. By framing the manufacturing white paper through a sustainability lens for this campaign, we ride that traffic spike and reach edie's sustainability director readership when they are most engaged.

This document covers edie only. Separate deadline schedules will follow for The Energyst, The Manufacturer, and the wider content cascade.

WHY THESE DATES

Every publish date below was recommended by Laura at edie. She knows when each format spikes for her audience. The day of LCAW is not always the best moment to publish long-form content, attention is split between live event coverage and on-demand reading.

The thought leadership blog in the first week of July is timed deliberately to support the month-long banner. The two assets work together, compounding visibility through July rather than spiking on a single day.
